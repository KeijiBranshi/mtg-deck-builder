// Description: Based on the Command Zone's deck building methodology. Cards can belong to multiple categories, so slot totals (108) exceed the 100-card deck size. When tagging a card, apply all categories that fit -- e.g. Rishkar's Expertise is both #Ramp and #CardAdvantage.

Ramp: 10
  Cards that accelerate mana production ahead of curve: mana rocks (Sol Ring),
  mana dorks (Llanowar Elves), land ramp spells (Cultivate), cost reducers,
  and rituals.

CardAdvantage: 12
  Cards that generate net card advantage or meaningful card selection.
  Includes hard draw (Rhystic Study, Harmonize), repeatable draw engines
  (Phyrexian Arena), impulse/exile draw (Light Up the Stage), tutors and
  recursion engines, token generators that replace themselves, and scry/
  surveil effects that improve card quality. Pure draw is the strongest
  form, but scry/surveil still count since they reduce dead draws. Weight
  draw effects more heavily when evaluating whether this category is full.

TargetedDisruption: 12
  Cards that answer a single specific threat: spot removal (Swords to
  Plowshares), counterspells (Counterspell), targeted exile (Anguished
  Unmaking), targeted discard, single-permanent bounce, edicts, stun
  counters, and single-target -X/-X effects (Vilis's activated ability,
  Disfigure). If an effect hits multiple targets at once, it belongs in
  MassDisruption instead.

MassDisruption: 6
  Cards that affect multiple permanents, cards, or resources at once.
  This is a spectrum: full board wipes (Wrath of God, Farewell) have the
  broadest blast radius; asymmetric wipes (Cyclonic Rift, Plague Wind)
  hit opponents but spare you; narrow mass removal (Vandalblast
  overloaded, mass -X/-X) hits a specific card type across the board;
  targeted-but-mass effects (Bojuka Bog, Tormod's Crypt) target one
  player but remove MANY of their cards. All count toward the 6-slot
  target, but a deck leaning narrow may want to run closer to the high
  end. Prefer at least 2-3 true board wipes among the 6.

Land: 38
  Mana-producing lands in the deck.

Plan:
  Cards that directly support your deck's primary strategy and win
  condition. These define what your deck is trying to DO. Varies
  entirely by commander/strategy, so no fixed count target -- treat
  this category as the budget for strategy-specific cards after the
  other categories are filled.
